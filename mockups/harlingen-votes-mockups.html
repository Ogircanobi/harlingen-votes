Methodology & corrections

Where the data comes from

Every record on this site comes from the City of Harlingen's public meeting portal (harlingentx.portal.civicclerk.com). The City does not publish minutes as standalone documents — approved minutes appear inside the agenda packet of a later meeting, usually two to four months afterward. We locate each set of minutes, extract the text (many packets are scanned paper, read by OCR), and pull out each motion: who moved, who seconded, the outcome, and the roll call when one was recorded. Extraction is done with AI assistance and checked two ways:

  • Every extracted vote keeps a verbatim quote from the minutes, automatically matched back against the source document.
  • A random sample of motions is compared by hand against the source PDFs at every update. Results of each audit are posted here.

Latest audit (July 2026): 30 randomly sampled motions checked against source PDFs; 30 of 30 outcomes and tallies matched; 2 name spellings corrected for OCR artifacts.

What the marks mean

* — The minutes say "motion carried unanimously" without listing names. We show the members recorded present that day and mark each attributed vote with an asterisk. The City did not record their votes individually.
Absent — The member was recorded absent for the meeting and cast no vote on the item.
Name rows — Columns are seats (Mayor, District 1–5), because officials change and seats don't. A blue name row shows who held each seat for the votes beneath it and stays pinned while you scroll; a yellow-highlighted name marks a seat that changed hands at that point.

Missing meetings

If the City has not yet posted minutes for a meeting, that meeting is listed with the note "minutes not yet posted" rather than silently left out. 22 meetings currently fall in that category.
